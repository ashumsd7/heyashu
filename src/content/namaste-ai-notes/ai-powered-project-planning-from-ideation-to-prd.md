### Summary of our project

**Make My Marriage** is an all-in-one wedding management platform for Indian weddings. One account manages one wedding, with the groom/bride as the primary user and multiple family members or organizers joining the same wedding using their own Google accounts. An Admin manages the wedding and members, while guests do not need to log in.

The platform provides a central **Wedding Dashboard** to manage events, guests, expenses, tasks, shopping, vendors, invitations, photos, and live streams. Default events such as **Haldi, Mehndi, Sangeet, Wedding, and Reception** are provided, while Admins can create custom events. Each event can have its own guests, vendors, tasks, expenses, photos, and other details.

Guests are maintained in a central guest collection and can be assigned to specific events with RSVP tracking. Users can discover nearby vendors such as photographers, caterers, decorators, tent houses, hotels, etc., and attach vendors to one or multiple events.

Every wedding automatically gets a **public wedding website**, digital invitation, RSVP page, gallery, and live-stream section. Members and guests can upload photos, including through a QR-code-based upload page, while galleries remain private unless a shareable link is generated.

The product will also have a public **landing page** and an interactive **"Ashu's Marriage" demo wedding** where anyone can explore how the complete platform works.

### Let’s create the PRD (**Product Requirements Document**)

![](/images/namaste-ai/img-3-s2-prd.png)

In business and technology, **PRD** stands for **Product Requirements Document**. In easy words, a PRD is a **written blueprint** for building a new product or software feature. It acts as a single guide that explains **what** is being built, **why** it is being made, and **how** it should work.

It’s built by the project and engineering team as they brainstorm and build the feature, so let’s create that PRD.

Now your task is to ask your ChatGPT or whatever you are using and download the PRD.

> I’m okay with the requirements and all. Please create a PRD document for me.

Once the PRD is downloaded, read it and ask for any modification if required, and again do the same.
