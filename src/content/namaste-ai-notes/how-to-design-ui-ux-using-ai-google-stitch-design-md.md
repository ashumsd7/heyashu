### What is Google Stitch?

**Google Stitch** is **an AI-powered UI design tool from Google Labs that turns text prompts, sketches, or screenshots into responsive user interfaces and front-end code**. It helps you build multi-screen mockups and design systems quickly without requiring advanced design skills.

[Visit Google Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/)

### What is **DESIGN.md** file?

**DESIGN.md** file is **a simple text cheat sheet that tells AI tools and coding assistants how your app or website should look**.

Think of it like hiring a new designer and handing them a one-page rulebook before they start. Instead of guessing your style, the AI reads this file to know your exact colors, fonts, spacing rules, and design choices.
